Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-26 · Developer: Hakan Şen

Summary

Last Z Auto Helper does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything it does happens on your computer.

What the app does on your computer

  • It captures images of the Last Z game window to find the alliance help button. These images are processed in memory and are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • It clicks the help button with the mouse and restores your cursor and active window.
  • It saves your settings (language, shortcut, preferences) and a small log file in your user folder (%APPDATA%\LastZAutoHelper). These files never leave your computer.

Network access

  • Microsoft Store version: the app makes no network requests; updates are delivered by the Microsoft Store.
  • Version downloaded from the website: the app can check for a new version by downloading a small public file (surum.json) from GitHub Pages, at most once a day. No personal data is sent. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. You can turn this off in Settings.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
